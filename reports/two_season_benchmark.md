# Two-season benchmark (Snowline, 2025–26)

Source: https://snowline.jp/ja/season/recap/2025-26

Snowline tracks 383 ski areas; 360 reported season periods. Among 346 resorts with openings in both seasons, 148 opened later, 35 earlier, and 163 within three days of the prior season. For 318 comparable ski areas, average snowfall was 26% lower than 2024–25, and average maximum snow depth was 25% lower. Average days with snow depth at least 100 cm fell from 86 to 53.

**Interpretation limits:** One season-to-season difference cannot be attributed to climate warming; operating dates are not actual operating days. The sample is not the 417-facility official roster. Source's regional groupings may differ from official boundaries.

Research value: This provides a testable year-over-year signal and suggests obtaining longer historical series and matched land-price points before inference.
