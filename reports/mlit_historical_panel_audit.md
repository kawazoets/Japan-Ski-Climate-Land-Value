The 2026 archive supplies historical annual price fields L01_062–L01_105. The L01_061 selection string is preserved for audit. A zero or missing value is excluded rather than interpreted as a zero land price. Historical records are attached to the 2026 survey-point sample: sites that disappeared before 2026 may be absent (survivorship bias). Do not use this panel as a balanced, representative municipality price index without validating survey-point continuity and selection.
