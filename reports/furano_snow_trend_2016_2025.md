# Furano official JMA snow-depth trends: 2016–2025 winter starts

Station 12940 (富良野), observed November–April; ten complete winters.

| Measure | 2016 winter | 2025 winter | 2016–20 mean | 2021–25 mean | Linear slope per year |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Snow cover >=1cm (days) | 154.0 | 118.0 | 138.8 | 122.0 | -3.03 |
| Snow cover >=30cm (days) | 110.0 | 68.0 | 95.2 | 84.2 | -3.15 |
| Maximum daily snow depth (cm) | 88.0 | 67.0 | 91.2 | 76.6 | -2.95 |
| Mean daily snow depth (cm) | 37.7 | 22.4 | 33.0 | 27.0 | -1.55 |

## Interpretation limits

- The slope is an unadjusted ordinary least squares descriptive estimate over only ten winter seasons; no significance or causal claim is made.
- A valley station is not necessarily representative of the mountain ski slopes, even if geographically nearby.
- Snow-cover days are measured at the weather station, **not** days a resort was open.
- No resort operating-day evidence is yet linked; no snow-to-land-price inference is supported.
- Only November–April is counted; any May or October operating days are excluded.

Source: `data/jma_snow_winter_season_metrics_2016_2025.csv` and official JMA daily observations in `data/jma_daily_snow_depth_2016_2026.csv`.
